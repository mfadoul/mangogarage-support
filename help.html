Using Mango Garage

How tire pressure status is shown, what the weather features do, and how to use widgets and shortcuts.

Tire pressure status

Mango Garage compares each tire's latest reading with the recommended cold pressure you enter for the car. To add it, open the car, choose Edit Vehicle, and fill in Tires. Use the label inside the driver's door (the placard) or the owner's manual, not the maximum pressure printed on the tire's sidewall.

StatusColorDifference from recommended
Near recommendedGreenWithin 5%
Slightly offYellow5–10% low, or 5–15% high
Well offRedMore than 10% low, or more than 15% high
UnknownGrayNo reading for that tire, or no recommended pressure to compare with

History rows also use a different shape for each status, so the meaning never depends on color alone. These ranges describe how far a reading is from the number you entered; they are not a safety rating. Follow your owner's manual and a qualified professional's advice about your tires.

A color needs both a recorded tire check and a recommended pressure. When one is missing, the Car Status widget says which: No tire pressures recorded, No recommended pressure set, or No recorded or recommended pressures.

Weather

Tire pressure changes with air temperature, so a tire check can record the temperature. Tap the weather button in a tire-pressure entry to look it up for the check's date, time, and place. Weather comes from Apple Weather and is credited wherever it appears.

Weather reminders for tire pressure in Settings is off by default. When you turn it on, the app checks the temperature near you when you open it, at most once every six hours, and lets you know when it has changed enough since your last check to affect your readings. It never runs in the background. See how weather lookups handle your location.

Widgets and shortcuts

To add a widget, touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Mango Garage.

  • Car Status shows a car's nickname, photo, latest calculated fuel economy, and tire status. It shows your favorite car unless you choose another: touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget. Tap it to open that car's page.
  • Quick Actions starts a fuel event or a tire pressure check for your favorite car, or your first car if you haven't chosen a favorite.
  • Add Fuel and Tire Pressure are also available on the Lock Screen, in Control Center, and as Siri and Shortcuts actions. Settings → Siri & Shortcuts lists the phrases Siri recognizes.

The Car Status widget updates when you open Mango Garage or leave it, so a change you just saved appears there right away. Anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen can see what a widget shows.
