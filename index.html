Support for your vehicle records.

Mango Garage is a local-first vehicle log. You can record vehicles, fuel events, and tire pressure checks manually without creating an account. Cloud Assist is an optional photo-analysis feature that always returns suggestions for your review.

Private development testing. Mango Garage is not yet publicly released. This site is prepared so its stable links, support process, and public disclosures can be reviewed before release. Do not treat a draft policy or planned account-deletion workflow as a live public service.

How we can help

Use Mango Garage locally

Manual records remain on your device. Apple sign-in is only requested when you intentionally choose Cloud Assist. Local records can be deleted separately in Settings without deleting Cloud Assist access.

Back up and move fuel history

Use a ZIP archive for a full local backup. Use CSV or XLSX to work with fuel history in a spreadsheet. Guided import from CSV, XLSX, or ODS is intentionally narrower than a full restore.

Cloud Assist

Cloud Assist analyzes selected fuel-event photos and presents possible values for you to review. It does not automatically save a fuel event, and manual entry remains available.
