Delete Cloud Assist access or local data

Cloud Assist identity and local vehicle data are deliberately separate. Each deletion action has a different scope, and neither silently triggers the other.

Not available yet. Mango Garage is in private development testing, and deleting a Cloud Assist account from the app is not available yet. This page describes how it will work. Please don't send account details by email.

What will be deleted

When it is available, deletion will remove your Cloud Assist account and its records, including your scan usage and any purchases, and the scan results kept to protect retries.

Some limited records may have to be kept for legal or security reasons. The final privacy policy will say which ones and for how long.

What will stay local unless you separately choose otherwise

  • Vehicle profiles, fuel, maintenance, and tire records stored on your device.
  • Photos and locally retained originals stored on your device.
  • ZIP backups or spreadsheet exports you previously shared or stored.

Before deleting Cloud Assist access, export anything you want to keep. Mango Garage has a separate, clearly labeled Delete Local Mango Garage Data choice in Settings. It requires its own final confirmation and does not delete your Cloud Assist identity or server-side access.

Delete local Mango Garage data

  1. In the app, open Settings → Local Data → Delete Local Mango Garage Data.
  2. Review the local vehicle, event, and photo counts.
  3. Choose the destructive action and confirm once more.

This removes vehicle profiles, fuel and service events, event photos, vehicle photos, and retained full-quality originals from that device. It keeps app preferences, exports or backups you stored elsewhere, and Cloud Assist access. Local deletion cannot be undone.

Delete your Cloud Assist account (when available)

  1. Open Settings → Cloud Assist → Cloud Assist Access → Delete Cloud Assist Account.
  2. Review what will be deleted, and export anything you want to keep.
  3. Confirm by signing in with Apple again.
  4. The app deletes your Cloud Assist account and signs you out, then offers the separate choice to delete local data.

Why an email request is not enough

Deleting an account requires signing in with Apple again from the app, which an email cannot do safely. Please don't email Apple credentials, account identifiers, photos, or full vehicle-identification numbers.

If the released in-app deletion flow fails, it will show a retry and support option. For non-sensitive questions during private testing, email papayamedley.app@gmail.com.
